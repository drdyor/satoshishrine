What we do not hold

This panel is where a dashboard would normally put an aggregate regulator metric. None is held, and this is more useful.

  • Direct taxation is national competence. No tax rate, holding period or filing deadline is held for ANY jurisdiction, and none is shown.
  • The non-compliant register is not a harmonised EU blacklist: 165 of 167 entries are IT. Absence from it outside IT means the register is quiet, not that an entity is clean.
  • National licensing procedure (which NCA, what the application demands) is not held for any Member State.
