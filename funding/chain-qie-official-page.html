How is QIE different from Bitcoin and Ethereum? Bitcoin: ~6 TPS, designed as digital gold. Ethereum: ~30 TPS, scalable but expensive fees. QIE: 25,000 TPS, ultra-low fees, and structural deflation via gas-fee burning. QIE is not just a payment system but an operating system for dApps, combining speed, utility, and governance. What makes QIE’s tokenomics unique? Hard cap: 150 million tokens. Deflationary design: 80% of all gas fees are burned.Validator rewards: Secured by slashing penalties to discourage bad behavior. This model ties network activity directly to token scarcity, creating long-term value. How secure is QIE? QIE combines Proof-of-Stake with slashing. Validators earn rewards for securing the network but lose staked tokens if they act maliciously. This incentive + penalty mechanism creates a robust security model.
